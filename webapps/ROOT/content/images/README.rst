Use of MedCyprus Images
----

Images on this website are reproduced with permission for use within the MedCyprus project. This permission does not extend to third-party use. For permission to reproduce or otherwise use a MedCyprus image, please contact the Department of Antiquities of Cyprus AND the relevant repository or ecclesiastical authority (listed under “Diocese” in individual entries on the `Locations page <https://medcyprus.ucy.ac.cy/en/indices/epidoc/locations.html>`__ ). For permission to reproduce or otherwise use an archival image featured on this website, please contact the relevant institution directly. For enquiries regarding permission to reproduce or otherwise use an image from a personal archive featured on this website, please contact the MedCyprus coordinator: parani.maria@ucy.ac.cy
